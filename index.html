About

Three days in Bochum bringing together the PPiMP network, the DFG-funded research network for predictive processing in memory and perception. Guests are warmly invited to join. Please register your attendance.

The programme features talks, keynotes, and discussions. It opens with a welcome reception and a poster session on Tuesday evening and closes on Thursday at midday.
